### Company Overview
**Eyebrow:** Who we are  
**Headline:** About French Connection Wines

French Connection Wines is an importation and distribution company based in Hội An, Vietnam. We were founded with a clear passion: to bring the taste of authentic French wine to Vietnam, and to make the best of France's wine-growing heritage accessible to a new audience.

Our vision is to become the first company in Vietnam entirely specialized in French wine — offering a diverse, carefully curated range of bottles from the famous appellations of the South of France that have captivated wine lovers around the world.

We work directly with producers and cooperatives committed to quality and environmental responsibility. Several of our wines carry the High Environmental Value Level 3 certification — France's highest standard for sustainable viticulture.

### Wine Production Philosophy (Process Section)
**Eyebrow:** How our wines are made  
**Headline:** From Terroir to Table

**Harvesting**  
Our producers harvest each variety at peak maturity — the foundational decision that determines everything else. Several estates practice manual harvesting to preserve the integrity of each bunch.

**Fermentation**  
Temperature-controlled fermentation locks in the aromatic freshness and character specific to each grape variety and appellation. No shortcuts at this stage.

**Clarification & Aging**  
Each wine undergoes its own maturation protocol — from early-drinking Pays d'Oc cuvées to the structured Chardonnay of AOP Limoux and the celebrated blends of AOP Cabardès. Patience is part of the process.

**Bottling & Import**  
Once bottled at the estate, wines are imported under controlled conditions to ensure they arrive in Vietnam exactly as the winemaker intended. The bottle you open is the bottle they made.
